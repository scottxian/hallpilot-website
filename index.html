Features

Everything schools need. Nothing they don’t.

Hall Pilot keeps the student experience simple while giving staff the controls and visibility they need.

Student-friendly requests

Students can quickly request a pass, return from a pass, and see clear status updates.

Teacher control panel

Approve, return, block, override, and manage student passes with a live classroom dashboard.

Smart rules and limits

Configure restroom pass limits, waiting lists, lockouts, destinations, schedules, and emergency controls.

Reporting and visibility

View pass history, student activity, daily reports, and classroom trends in one place.

School and organization ready

Support multiple rooms, schools, and organizations while keeping their data properly separated.

Web-based deployment

Accessible from modern browsers, easy to update, and simple to roll out to staff and students.

How it works

Simple flow. Clear accountability.

01

Student requests a pass

The student selects a destination and submits a request from the room page.

02

Teacher reviews and approves

The teacher sees the request instantly and can approve, deny, or manage the queue.

03

Return is recorded automatically

When the student returns, the pass is closed and included in reports and activity history.

Why schools choose it

Built to be practical, not flashy.

Hall Pilot is designed around what educators actually need: fewer interruptions, quick decisions, clearer accountability, and simpler schoolwide management.

It works well for a single classroom and scales toward larger school or multi-school use without changing the core experience.

  • Clear student and staff interfaces
  • Custom schedules and room-level controls
  • Teacher and admin dashboards
  • Quick setup for rooms, students, and destinations
  • Professional branding and browser-based access
