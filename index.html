Features

What teachers and school staff can do.

Pass requests are simple for students. The rules and records stay with the staff.

Student passes and waiting lists

Students choose an available destination, request a pass, and check in when they return. If a destination is full, they join the waiting list.

Live teacher dashboard

See Students Out and Waiting List side by side. Open or close restroom passes, record returns, and issue overrides when needed.

Limits and exceptions

Set student restrictions by period, day, week, or month. Control destinations and record teacher overrides rather than bypassing the rules silently.

Bell schedules and special days

Assign a primary schedule, then set minimum days or special schedules by date, room, or grade. Student and teacher screens use the same active schedule.

Schoolwide controls

Administrators manage rooms, staff assignments, and destinations. Emergency lock stops new passes across the campus when needed.

Reports and CSV exports

Review pass history by student, room, destination, or date. Verified returns and administrative close-outs are counted separately. Export records to CSV.

Phone-friendly access

Hall Pilot on your home screen.

Open your school's Student, Teacher, or Admin page from your phone and add it to your home screen. Each shortcut has the Hall Pilot icon and its own name: Hall Pass Student, Hall Pass Teacher, or Hall Pass Admin.

No app-store download is needed. The shortcut keeps the school or classroom link you opened, so you're back on the right page with one tap.

How to add it

  1. Open the Hall Pilot link provided by your school.
  2. iPhone: In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    Android: In Chrome, open the menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Check the name and tap Add.

Internet access is still required for live pass information.

How it works

Four steps, from request to return.

01

Student requests a pass

The student selects an available destination on the classroom screen.

02

Hall Pilot checks the rules

The active bell schedule, student restrictions, and destination capacity determine what happens next.

03

Go or wait

If space is available, the pass can begin. Otherwise, the student waits and selects Go when eligible. Some destinations need teacher approval.

04

Return is recorded

The student checks back in. If a pass is closed administratively instead, reports do not count that as a verified return.

Built around school schedules

Set the regular day once. Handle exceptions as they come.

A room can keep its primary bell schedule while the school applies minimum days, special events, or a different schedule for selected grades. Staff don't have to rebuild the calendar every morning.

Teachers control their own rooms. Administrators can set schoolwide rules and review activity without interrupting class.

  • Primary schedules for rooms or groups
  • Minimum days and date-specific exceptions
  • Room-level destination settings
  • Student restrictions and documented overrides
  • Teacher reports and campus reporting
